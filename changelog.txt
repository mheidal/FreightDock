========================
2026-11-03 v1.1

Bugfixes
- Haulers no longer have goods turned away at a freight dock that asked for them.
- "Keep empty" warehouses set as "Deliver to" now have their needs shipped.
- Ships leaving and returning to their home berth turn in an arc instead of turning in place, where there is room.
- Loading requests are now limited by contents of "Pick up from" warehouses. The split of loading requests by good is proportional to the request from the partner dock.
- A ship that is ready to leave waits up to 30 s for goods still being carried to its dock.

Changes
- A resource no hauler has picked up for 20 s is paused for 60 s, so its space goes to other resources.
- Removed "Load resources onto ship" UI panel for freight docks.
- Removed departure checklist UI element from game's freight dock UI.

Features
- The Freight Dock has its own technology.
- Freight Dock panel beside the dock window:
- - partner freight dock ID (click to pan to dock)
- - ship hold contents and trip time
- - goods waiting to be unloaded
- - a table of what the partner wants (aboard, being carried, unloaded, still to fetch, wanted)
- - linked warehouses (click to pan to warehouse)
- - "Deliver now" button
- When a freight ship arrives, its goods fly from the ship to the dock.
