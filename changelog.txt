2026-11-11 v1.0

Features
- Freight Docks now support many-to-many deliveries according to a network structure, rather than one-to-one pairings.
- A dispatcher system assigns delivery jobs between Freight Docks on the same network, prioritizing larger deliveries and shorter trips, with weighting to ensure that no requester is neglected.
- Freight Docks can join networks as providers, requesters, or both. Deliveries are scheduled from providers to requesters.
- Freight Docks can join up to 3 networks.
- A single save file can have up to 31 networks.
- 1.x save files are converted to the network system on load. A save file with the 2.0 format cannot be converted to the 1.x format.
- Various displays of network connectivity.
- A minimap showing Freight Docks on each network.
- A new GUI panel to control Freight Docks network membership, and show which goods are requested on each network.
- Jobs can now survive a save-and-load cycle.
- Settings can be copied and pasted between Freight Docks. This copies network membership.
- Freight Dock's GUI panel shows: 
- - thorough diagnosis of why deliveries are not being made.
- - goods available to ship.
- - manifest for assigned job.
- - total requests at target of assigned job.
- A Freight Dock can be paused, preventing the assignment of future jobs until unpaused.
- Freight Docks and networks can be renamed.
- New setting, "Minimum load". Keeps ships from being assigned deliveries which would fill too little of their hold. Deliveries still assigned to requesters which have waited long enough.
- Selecting a dock draws arcs connecting it to docks it trades with, color-coded by network.
- Requesters and providers are color-coded throughout the interface, red for providers and blue for requesters.

Changes
- The F5 pairing key has been removed.
- Deliveries are only assigned to providers which have input warehouses linked.
- A Freight Dock which is both a provider and a requester does not deliver goods which its output warehouses request.
- Several Freight Docks can be linked to the same input or output warehouses without double-counting.
- "Depart after" setting can now be set to "Never".
- "Deliver now" causes the ship to depart immediately rather than waiting for the final hauler trip to complete.

Optimizations
- Route planning is spread across many frames to reduce hitches.
- Route planning reacts faster to placement of buildings.

Bugfixes
- Goods can be dropped from job manifests if they are unavailable or unreachable.

2026-11-03 v1.1

Bugfixes
- Haulers no longer have goods turned away at a freight dock that asked for them.
- "Keep empty" warehouses set as "Deliver to" now have their needs shipped.
- Ships leaving and returning to their home berth turn in an arc instead of turning in place, where there is room.
- Loading requests are now limited by contents of "Pick up from" warehouses. The split of loading requests by good is proportional to the request from the partner dock.
- A ship that is ready to leave waits up to 30 s for goods still being carried to its dock.

Changes
- A resource no hauler has picked up for 20 s is paused for 60 s, so its space goes to other resources.
- Removed "Load resources onto ship" UI panel for freight docks.
- Removed departure checklist UI element from game's freight dock UI.

Features
- The Freight Dock has its own technology.
- Freight Dock panel beside the dock window:
- - partner freight dock ID (click to pan to dock)
- - ship hold contents and trip time
- - goods waiting to be unloaded
- - a table of what the partner wants (aboard, being carried, unloaded, still to fetch, wanted)
- - linked warehouses (click to pan to warehouse)
- - "Deliver now" button
- When a freight ship arrives, its goods fly from the ship to the dock.
